# Chargeback and Payment Dispute Policy

## Definition
A chargeback occurs when a customer disputes a charge directly with their bank or card issuer rather than contacting NorthStar Retail support first. This is distinct from a standard refund request, which goes through support and the Return Policy.

## Internal Process
When a chargeback notice is received, the order is immediately flagged and any pending shipment is held. Support attempts to contact the customer to understand the dispute reason before the bank's response deadline (typically 7-10 business days).

## Encouraging Direct Resolution
Customers who file a chargeback instead of contacting support first should be encouraged to withdraw the dispute in favor of a direct refund or replacement, since chargebacks take longer to resolve and can result in a temporary hold on future orders while the dispute is open.

## Repeat Chargeback Customers
Accounts with 3 or more chargebacks in a 12-month period are flagged for manual review before future orders are shipped, and may be restricted to prepaid-only payment methods.

## Fraud Distinction
Chargebacks citing "unauthorized transaction" are treated as potential fraud and are escalated separately from service-quality disputes (e.g., "item not as described").
