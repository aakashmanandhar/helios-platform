# Damaged or Defective Item Policy

## Scope
This policy covers items that arrive physically damaged (crushed box, cracked casing, visible shipping damage) or that are defective out of the box (doesn't power on, missing functionality described in the listing).

## No Return Shipping Fee
Damaged and defective items are always eligible for a free return or replacement - the $5.99 return shipping fee under the standard Return Policy does not apply here.

## Replacement vs. Refund
Customers can choose a replacement (shipped immediately, no need to wait for the damaged item to be received back) or a full refund. Replacements are shipped via expedited shipping at no cost.

## Evidence Requirements
For items damaged in transit, support may request 1-2 photos of the damaged packaging and product before approving the replacement, primarily to support the internal carrier damage claim, not to gatekeep the customer's request.

## Timing
Defective/damaged item claims should be filed within 30 days of delivery. Items that fail after 30 days are handled under the Warranty Policy instead.
