# Return Policy

## Standard Return Window
Most items purchased from NorthStar Retail may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method. The 30-day clock starts on the date the order status changes to "delivered," not the order date.

## Condition Requirements
Items must be returned unused, in original packaging, with all tags and accessories included. Items showing signs of use beyond inspection (e.g., visible wear, missing components) may be subject to a restocking fee of up to 15% of the item price, or the return may be declined.

## Non-Returnable Categories
The following categories are final sale and cannot be returned except for manufacturer defects covered under the Warranty Policy: perishable goods, personal care items that have been opened, and clearance/closeout items marked "Final Sale" at checkout.

## Return Shipping
Return shipping is free for defective or incorrectly shipped items. For change-of-mind returns, a flat $5.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund unless the customer holds Gold or Platinum loyalty status (see Loyalty Program policy), in which case return shipping is always free.

## Refund Timing
Refunds are issued within 3-5 business days of the returned item passing warehouse inspection. Refund to the original payment method can take an additional 2-7 business days to appear depending on the customer's bank.

## Late Returns
Return requests submitted after the 30-day window are evaluated case-by-case by a support agent and are not guaranteed. Orders with documented shipping delays (see Shipping Policy) may have their return window extended by the number of days the shipment was late.
