# Loyalty Program

## Tiers
- **Silver** (default): 1 point per $1 spent.
- **Gold** (reached at $500 in trailing-12-month spend): 1.5 points per $1 spent, free return shipping.
- **Platinum** (reached at $1,500 in trailing-12-month spend): 2 points per $1 spent, free return shipping, free expedited shipping on all orders, and early access to sales.

## Redeeming Points
Points can be redeemed at checkout in increments of 500 points = $5 off. Points do not expire as long as the account has at least one purchase every 12 months; otherwise points expire on the account's anniversary date.

## Tier Downgrades
Tier status is recalculated on a rolling 12-month basis. A customer who drops below the spend threshold for their tier is downgraded at the start of the next calendar quarter, with a notification email sent 2 weeks in advance.

## Combining with Other Offers
Loyalty points can be combined with most promo codes, but not with clearance/Final Sale pricing or price-match adjustments.
