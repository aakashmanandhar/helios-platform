# Shipping Policy

## Standard Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days and is free on orders over $35. Orders under $35 incur a flat $4.99 shipping fee.

## Expedited Shipping
Expedited shipping (1-2 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee based on package weight, typically $9.99-$19.99.

## Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1 business day of being placed, except during peak periods (Black Friday through New Year's Day), when processing may take up to 3 business days.

## Late Shipment Policy
An order is considered "late" if it has not arrived within the estimated delivery window shown at checkout. Customers with a late shipment are entitled to one of the following, their choice: a shipping fee refund (if a fee was charged), a $10 store credit, or an extended return window (see Return Policy).

## Lost Packages
If tracking shows "delivered" but the customer has not received the package, support should wait 48 hours (in case of a carrier scanning error) before opening a lost package investigation. If the package is not located within 5 business days of the investigation opening, the order is treated as lost and a full refund or replacement is issued.

## Damaged in Transit
Packages that arrive visibly damaged should be handled under the Damaged/Defective Item Policy, not this policy - carrier damage claims are filed internally by support, not the customer.
