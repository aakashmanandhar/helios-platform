# Warranty Policy

## Standard Manufacturer Warranty
Most electronics and appliances sold by NorthStar Retail carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, starting from the delivery date. Warranty claims are handled directly with the manufacturer for items still under warranty after the 30-day return window has closed.

## NorthStar Extended Coverage
Customers who purchased optional Extended Coverage at checkout receive an additional 1-2 years of coverage (product-dependent) that also covers accidental damage, which manufacturer warranties typically exclude.

## What's Covered
Defects that occur under normal use: components failing prematurely, manufacturing flaws, and items that stop functioning without physical damage caused by the customer.

## What's Not Covered
Damage from misuse, unauthorized repairs, water damage (unless the product is marketed as waterproof and used within spec), and cosmetic wear that doesn't affect function.

## Filing a Warranty Claim
Within the first 30 days, warranty issues are treated as a standard return/replacement (see Return Policy) and don't require proof of the defect beyond the customer's description. After 30 days, support may request photos or a video of the issue before approving a replacement or repair.
