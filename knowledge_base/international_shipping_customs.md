# International Shipping and Customs Policy

## Current Coverage
NorthStar Retail currently ships internationally to Canada, the UK, and the EU. All other regions, including APO/FPO military addresses, are not currently supported for direct shipment.

## Customs and Duties
International orders may be subject to customs duties and import taxes charged by the destination country, which are the customer's responsibility and are not included in the checkout price.

## Delivery Times
International standard shipping takes 7-14 business days, longer than domestic shipping, and does not qualify for the domestic Late Shipment Policy remedies - a separate international delivery window applies.

## Returns on International Orders
International returns follow the standard Return Policy, but the customer is responsible for return shipping costs regardless of the reason, since NorthStar does not currently provide prepaid international return labels.
