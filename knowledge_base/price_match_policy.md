# Price Match Policy

## Eligibility
NorthStar Retail will match a lower price found at a small set of major named competitors, provided the item is identical (same SKU/model), in stock at the competitor, and the price includes shipping costs in the comparison.

## Timing
Price match requests must be submitted within 7 days of the NorthStar order date. Price drops on NorthStar's own site within that same 7-day window are also honored automatically as a courtesy credit.

## Exclusions
Price matching does not apply to: third-party marketplace sellers, clearance/Final Sale items, membership-only warehouse club pricing, or flash sales/limited-quantity deals.

## How Credits Are Issued
Approved price match differences are issued as a refund to the original payment method, not store credit, within 3-5 business days of approval.
